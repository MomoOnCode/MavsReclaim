Frequently Asked Questions

How Do I Access The Lockers If I Am Reporting An Item?

Fill out the Report a Found Item form and tell us which building you found it in. We reserve a free locker in that building right away, and the confirmation page shows you the locker number and a 4-digit PIN — we also email both to you. Enter the PIN on the locker keypad, place the item inside, and close the door. If every locker in that building is already full, we'll tell you to take the item to the Lost and Found Office instead.

How Do I Access The Lockers If I Am Claiming An Item?

Fill out the Report a Lost Item form describing what you lost, where, and when. An admin compares your report against the items currently stored on campus. Once they confirm a match, we email you the building, the locker number, and the PIN for that locker — enter it on the keypad to collect your item. If the item was held at the Lost and Found Office rather than a locker, bring your student ID to the office instead. You'll only hear from us once a match is approved.
